# Elite+ content plan (Arabic first)

Goal: bring patients from Google to the free assessment. Each article answers one real question, links to the treatment page and ends with the assessment and WhatsApp buttons.

Rules
- Written or reviewed by the medical team; each article shows the reviewing doctor's name.
- Every claim must be verifiable. No superlatives, guarantees or "painless". Results vary.
- Choose the final keyword for each article with Google Keyword Planner and Search Console data for the target country. The topics below are starting points, not measured search volumes.
- One article per week in Arabic; translate the best performers into EN, TR, DE and ES with native review. German and Spanish readers search with their own terms, so pick their keywords separately; don't just translate the Arabic ones.

## Months 4–6: comparison and trust

- زراعة الشعر في الندبات والحروق: متى تكون ممكنة
- العلاجات التجديدية للشعر (الخلايا الجذعية، G-Cell، ريجينيرا): ما الفرق وما الذي يقوله الطبيب
- كيف تقرأ صور قبل وبعد بشكل صحيح
- أسئلة يجب أن تطرحها على أي عيادة قبل زراعة الشعر
- زراعة الشعر للنساء: الأسباب والخيارات
- One doctor interview per month (per clinic): the doctor explains one topic on video.

## Months 7–12: seasonal and follow-up

- Best time of year to travel for treatment (by country of origin)
- Month 3, 6 and 12 results explained with consented patient cases
- Aftercare products and habits after transplant (medical team to approve)
- Update the top 5 articles every 6 months with new data and cases

## Measuring

- Search Console: impressions, clicks and position per article and language.
- GA4: `quiz_start` and `lead_submit` from blog traffic (landing page = article).
- Dashboard: requests and bookings by `landing_page` and `utm_campaign`.
