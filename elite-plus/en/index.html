Our techniques

FUE & DHI the right technique for you

Both are modern follicular unit techniques that leave no linear scar. Your doctor recommends one of them, or a combination, after examining your donor area, hair type and goals.

FUE Follicular Unit Extraction

Follicular units are extracted one by one from the donor area with a micro-punch. The doctor then opens tiny channels in the recipient area and places the grafts into them.

Often recommended for

  • Larger areas of thinning or baldness
  • A higher number of grafts in one session
  • Patients who are happy to shave the treatment area

DHI Direct Hair Implantation

Extraction is the same as in FUE. The difference is implantation: each graft is loaded into an implanter pen (Choi pen), which creates the channel and places the graft in a single step.

Often recommended for

  • Hairline design and adding density between existing hair
  • Beard, eyebrow and moustache transplants
  • Keeping the recipient area unshaved, when suitable

Good to know: DHI is a variation of FUE, not a competing operation. Studies have not shown that either technique reliably gives better results. Surgical skill, planning and graft handling matter most.

FAQ

Questions we hear often

How much does treatment cost?

Each treatment page shows a “starting from” price. Your final price depends on your medical assessment and is confirmed in a written quote before you travel. There are no hidden costs.

What is included in the package?

Packages typically include consultation and tests, the procedure, hotel stay, VIP transfers, an interpreter, medication and an aftercare kit, and remote follow-up. Your quote lists every item.

How long do I need to stay in Türkiye?

It depends on the treatment. Each treatment page shows the usual length of stay, and your coordinator confirms it in your plan.

Is the procedure painful?

Procedures are performed under appropriate anaesthesia. You may feel discomfort during recovery; your doctor will explain what to expect and prescribe medication if needed.

How long is the recovery?

Recovery time differs by treatment and by patient. Your doctor gives you a personal recovery timeline and written aftercare instructions.

When will I see the final results?

Results develop over time and vary between patients. Your doctor explains the expected timeline for your treatment during your consultation.

Is it safe to have treatment in Türkiye?

Safety depends on the clinic, not the country. Check the licence, the doctors' credentials and the hygiene standards. Ours are listed on the About & Safety and Doctors pages.

Do you speak my language?

Your coordinator communicates with you in your language, and an interpreter is with you at the clinic.
