Your journey, step by step

What happens from your first message to your follow-up at home.

  1. 1

    Free online assessment

    Answer a few questions and, if you wish, share photos. It takes about 3 minutes.

  2. 2

    Your plan & quote

    A doctor reviews your case and your coordinator sends a personalised plan with a written quote.

  3. 3

    Travel

    We book your hotel and meet you at the airport with a VIP transfer.

  4. 4

    Procedure day

    Consultation, tests and your procedure, with an interpreter at your side.

  5. 5

    Recovery

    Rest at your hotel with check-ups at the clinic before you fly home.

  6. 6

    Remote follow-up

    Photo check-ins and calls with your coordinator and doctor from home.

Before you travel

  • A valid passport (check visa rules for your nationality)
  • Your medical history and current medication list
  • Comfortable, loose clothing for your recovery
  • Travel insurance that covers medical treatment abroad
