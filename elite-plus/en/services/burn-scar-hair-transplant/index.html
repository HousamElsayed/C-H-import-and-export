Our techniques

FUE & DHI the right technique for you

Both are modern follicular unit techniques that leave no linear scar. Your doctor recommends one of them, or a combination, after examining your donor area, hair type and goals.

FUE Follicular Unit Extraction

Follicular units are extracted one by one from the donor area with a micro-punch. The doctor then opens tiny channels in the recipient area and places the grafts into them.

Often recommended for

  • Larger areas of thinning or baldness
  • A higher number of grafts in one session
  • Patients who are happy to shave the treatment area

DHI Direct Hair Implantation

Extraction is the same as in FUE. The difference is implantation: each graft is loaded into an implanter pen (Choi pen), which creates the channel and places the graft in a single step.

Often recommended for

  • Hairline design and adding density between existing hair
  • Beard, eyebrow and moustache transplants
  • Keeping the recipient area unshaved, when suitable

Good to know: DHI is a variation of FUE, not a competing operation. Studies have not shown that either technique reliably gives better results. Surgical skill, planning and graft handling matter most.
